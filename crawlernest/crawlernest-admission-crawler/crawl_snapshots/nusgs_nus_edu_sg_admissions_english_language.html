English Language Requirements

International applicants whose native language is not English must demonstrate English proficiency. NUS Graduate School accepts the following examinations:

TOEFL

The minimum accepted TOEFL iBT score is 85. Applicants who score below 85 may be offered conditional admission.

IELTS Academic

IELTS Academic minimum score: 6.0 overall. Minimum band scores: no individual band below 5.5.

Application for Masters and PhD

These requirements apply to both Masters (coursework and research) and doctoral (PhD) programmes offered by NUS Graduate School.

Deadlines

Applications for the August 2026 intake: deadline is 15 November 2025. Applications for January 2026 intake close on 15 July 2025.

NUS processes applications on a rolling basis.
