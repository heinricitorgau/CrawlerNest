English language requirements

All applicants for graduate study at Oxford who are not nationals of a majority English-speaking country (as defined by UKVI) must demonstrate their English language ability using one of the accepted tests below.

IELTS Academic

Most graduate courses at Oxford require a minimum overall IELTS score of 7.0, with a minimum of 6.5 in each component (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking).

Some courses in the Humanities or Social Sciences require IELTS 7.5 overall, with no component below 7.0. Check your course requirements carefully.

TOEFL iBT

The minimum TOEFL iBT score is 110 overall, with a minimum of 22 in each section. Oxford does not accept TOEFL MyBest scores for most courses.

Application closing dates

Oxford has multiple application deadlines depending on the course and funding competition. The earliest deadline is 15 October 2025 (for courses with October deadline), followed by a January round on 22 January 2026.

Level of study

These requirements apply to all postgraduate taught (MSc, MBA, MPhil) and research (DPhil/PhD) programmes.
