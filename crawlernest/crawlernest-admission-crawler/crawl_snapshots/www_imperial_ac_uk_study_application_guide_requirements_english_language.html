English Language Requirements

All applicants must meet English language requirements, unless they are a national of an English-speaking country or have recently completed a degree taught entirely in English.

Standard Tier — IELTS 6.5

Most postgraduate taught programmes require:

  • IELTS Academic: 6.5 overall, minimum 6.0 in each component
  • TOEFL iBT: 92 overall, minimum 20 in each section

Higher Tier — IELTS 7.0

Some programmes, including those in Medicine and certain Engineering specialisations, require:

  • IELTS Academic: 7.0 overall, minimum 6.5 in each component
  • TOEFL iBT: 100 overall, minimum 22 in each section

GPA

Applicants are expected to hold (or be predicted to hold) a UK First or upper second class honours degree, or international equivalent. This typically equates to a GPA of 3.5/4.0.

Application Deadlines

Applications for October 2026 entry should be submitted by 28 February 2026. Programmes with limited places may close earlier; some programmes accept applications on a rolling basis until filled.
