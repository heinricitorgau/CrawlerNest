MIT | Graduate Admissions | Apply | Requirements

English Language Requirements

MIT requires all applicants whose native language is not English to submit scores from a recognized English proficiency exam. Requirements vary by department.

TOEFL

Most MIT departments require a minimum TOEFL iBT score of 90. Some departments set a higher minimum of 100. The TOEFL home edition is accepted.

IELTS

MIT accepts IELTS Academic. The minimum score required is 7.0 overall. Some departments may require 7.5.

Waivers

Applicants who have completed or are completing a degree at an institution where English is the primary language of instruction may be eligible for a waiver. Contact your department directly.

Application Deadlines

Application deadlines vary by department. Most doctoral programs have a deadline of December 15, 2025. Masters programs typically close on January 1, 2026. Check your department's specific deadline.

Degree levels

These requirements apply to all graduate-level admissions including Masters (SM, MEng) and doctoral (PhD, ScD) programs.
