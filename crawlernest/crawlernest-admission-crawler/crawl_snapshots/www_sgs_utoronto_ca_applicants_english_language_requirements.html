English Language Requirements

Applicants whose first language is not English must provide evidence of proficiency in English as a condition of admission. The following minimum requirements apply to all graduate programs at U of T.

TOEFL

Minimum TOEFL iBT score: 93 overall. Individual sections: Writing 22, Speaking 22, Reading 22, Listening 22.

TOEFL scores must be received directly from ETS and must be no more than two years old.

IELTS

Minimum IELTS Academic score: 7.0 overall. No individual band below 6.5.

Duolingo English Test

The Duolingo English Test is accepted with a minimum score of 120.

GPA Equivalencies

Applicants are expected to have a minimum B+ average (GPA 3.3/4.0 or equivalent) from their most recent degree program.

Application Deadlines

Most graduate programs have an application deadline of January 15, 2026 for September 2026 entry. Please verify with your specific department as some have earlier deadlines.

Degree levels covered

These English language requirements apply to all graduate degree programs, including Masters and Doctoral degrees.
