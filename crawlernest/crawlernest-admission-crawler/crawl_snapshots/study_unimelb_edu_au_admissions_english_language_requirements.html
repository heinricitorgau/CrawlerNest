English language requirements for postgraduate study

If English is not your first language, you'll need to demonstrate English language proficiency as part of your application for most graduate-entry programs.

Standard requirements

For most postgraduate coursework and research degrees, the following minimum scores apply:

TestMinimum ScoreComponent Minimum
IELTS Academic6.5 overallNo band below 6.0
TOEFL iBT79Writing: 21, Speaking: 18
Duolingo English Test105N/A

Higher requirements

Some programs require IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 94. Check your program's specific requirements.

GPA requirements

A minimum GPA of 3.0/4.0 (or equivalent) is generally required for graduate study.

Application closing dates

The main application deadline for semester 1, 2026 intake is 30 October 2025. Late applications may be considered subject to availability.
