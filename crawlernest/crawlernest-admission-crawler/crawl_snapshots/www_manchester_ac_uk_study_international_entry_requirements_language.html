English Language Entry Requirements

If your first language is not English, you will need to provide evidence of your English language ability. We accept a variety of English language qualifications.

Standard Requirement

For the majority of our postgraduate programmes, the minimum requirement is:

  • IELTS: Overall 6.5, with no component below 6.0
  • TOEFL iBT: Overall 90, with minimum scores: Reading 22, Listening 21, Speaking 23, Writing 21
  • Duolingo English Test: minimum score of 120

Higher Requirements

Some courses, particularly in Medicine, Law, and Education, require IELTS 7.0 overall. Please check your specific course page for exact requirements.

GPA and Academic Requirements

Most taught masters programmes require a UK upper second-class honours degree (2:1) or international equivalent. For conversion, a minimum GPA of 3.0/4.0 is typically expected.

How to apply

Applications for September 2026 entry are accepted from October 2025. The main application deadline is 31 January 2026. Late applications may be considered if places are available.
