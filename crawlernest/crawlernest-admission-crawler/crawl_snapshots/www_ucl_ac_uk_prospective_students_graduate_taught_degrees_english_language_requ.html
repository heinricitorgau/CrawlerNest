English Language Requirements for Graduate Study

All applicants to UCL graduate programmes whose first language is not English must demonstrate proficiency in English. UCL accepts a range of English language qualifications.

Accepted Qualifications

IELTS (International English Language Testing System)

UCL requires a minimum overall IELTS score of 6.5, with no individual component (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking) below 6.0. Some programmes require higher scores.

For research degrees (MPhil, PhD), the requirement is a minimum overall IELTS score of 7.0, with no component below 6.5.

TOEFL iBT

The minimum TOEFL iBT score for UCL graduate programmes is 92 overall, with a minimum of 20 in each of the four sections. For research degrees, a score of 100 is required.

Duolingo English Test

UCL now accepts the Duolingo English Test with a minimum score of 120 for taught masters programmes.

Application Deadlines

Applications for most taught masters programmes should be submitted by March 31, 2026. Some popular programmes have earlier deadlines — please check individual programme pages.

For research degree applications, the deadline is rolling; however, most supervisors prefer applications received by January 15, 2026.

Degree Level Coverage

These requirements apply to all postgraduate programmes, including taught masters and MPhil/PhD research degrees.
